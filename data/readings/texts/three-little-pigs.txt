# The Three Little Pigs — English folktale (retold for graded reading)
# source: traditional, public domain

Once upon a time there were three little pigs. They lived with their mother in a small house in the country. One day their mother said, "You are big now. Go and build your own houses."

The first little pig was lazy. He built his house of straw. It was quick and easy. "Now I can play all day!" he said.

The second little pig built his house of sticks. It was a little stronger, but not very strong. "Now I can play too!" he said.

The third little pig worked hard all day. He built his house of bricks. It was slow work, but the house was very strong.

One night a big bad wolf came. He was hungry. He saw the house of straw and knocked on the door.

"Little pig, little pig, let me come in!" said the wolf.

"Not by the hair on my chinny chin chin!" said the first little pig.

"Then I'll huff, and I'll puff, and I'll blow your house in!" said the wolf.

And he huffed, and he puffed, and he blew the house of straw down. The first little pig ran as fast as he could to his brother's house of sticks.

The wolf came to the house of sticks. "Little pigs, little pigs, let me come in!" he said.

"Not by the hair on our chinny chin chins!" said the two little pigs.

"Then I'll huff, and I'll puff, and I'll blow your house in!" said the wolf.

And he huffed, and he puffed, and he blew the house of sticks down. The two little pigs ran as fast as they could to their brother's house of bricks.

The wolf came to the house of bricks. "Little pigs, little pigs, let me come in!" he said.

"Not by the hair on our chinny chin chins!" said the three little pigs.

"Then I'll huff, and I'll puff, and I'll blow your house in!" said the wolf.

And he huffed, and he puffed, and he huffed, and he puffed. But the house of bricks did not move. It was too strong.

The wolf was very angry. "I will come down the chimney and eat you all up!" he said.

The third little pig put a big pot of water on the fire. The water got very, very hot.

The wolf climbed up on the roof and jumped down the chimney. Splash! He fell right into the hot water. He jumped out, ran down the road, and never came back again.

The three little pigs laughed and danced. And the clever third little pig let his two brothers live with him in the strong brick house. They were safe and happy ever after.
