# Goldilocks and the Three Bears — English folktale (retold for graded reading)
# source: traditional, public domain

Once upon a time there were three bears. They lived in a little house in the forest. There was a great big Father Bear, a middle-sized Mother Bear, and a little Baby Bear.

One morning Mother Bear made porridge for breakfast. It was too hot to eat. "Let us go for a walk while it cools," said Father Bear. So off they went into the forest.

That same morning a little girl named Goldilocks was walking in the forest. She had golden hair, and that is why everyone called her Goldilocks. She saw the little house and knocked on the door. Nobody answered. She pushed the door. It opened, and in she went.

First, Goldilocks saw three bowls of porridge on the table. She was hungry. She tasted the porridge in the great big bowl. "Too hot!" she said. She tasted the porridge in the middle-sized bowl. "Too cold!" she said. She tasted the porridge in the little bowl. "Just right!" she said, and she ate it all up.

Next, Goldilocks saw three chairs. She sat in the great big chair. "Too hard!" she said. She sat in the middle-sized chair. "Too soft!" she said. She sat in the little chair. "Just right!" she said. But the little chair was not made for a girl. Crack! It broke into pieces.

Then Goldilocks went upstairs. She saw three beds. She lay on the great big bed. "Too hard!" she said. She lay on the middle-sized bed. "Too soft!" she said. She lay on the little bed. "Just right!" she said. And soon she fell fast asleep.

By and by the three bears came home. Father Bear looked at the table. "Somebody has been eating my porridge!" he said in his great big voice. "Somebody has been eating my porridge!" said Mother Bear in her middle-sized voice. "Somebody has been eating my porridge, and has eaten it all up!" said Baby Bear in his little small voice.

Then the bears looked at their chairs. "Somebody has been sitting in my chair!" said Father Bear. "Somebody has been sitting in my chair!" said Mother Bear. "Somebody has been sitting in my chair, and has broken it all to pieces!" said Baby Bear.

The three bears went upstairs. "Somebody has been lying on my bed!" said Father Bear. "Somebody has been lying on my bed!" said Mother Bear. "Somebody has been lying on my bed, and here she is!" said Baby Bear.

Goldilocks opened her eyes and saw the three bears looking at her. She jumped out of bed, ran down the stairs, and out of the door. She ran and ran through the forest until she got home. And she never went into anyone's house without knocking again.
